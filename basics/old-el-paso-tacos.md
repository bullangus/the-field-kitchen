Taco night from the kit: everyone builds their own, and the table ends up covered in lettuce.

## Ingredients

- 1 Old El Paso taco kit (shells, seasoning and salsa)
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) neutral oil
- 500 g beef mince
- ¼ iceberg lettuce, shredded
- 2 medium tomatoes, diced
- 100 g (1 cup) grated cheese
- 125 g (½ cup) sour cream

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a frying pan over high heat and brown the beef mince for 5–6 minutes, breaking it up.
2. Stir in the seasoning and the water the packet asks for, and simmer until thick.
3. Warm the shells in the oven, following the packet.
4. Put everything on the table and let everyone fill their own, with the salsa from the kit.

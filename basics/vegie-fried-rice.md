A bag of frozen vegies, eggs and a pot of rice tossed in a hot wok with soy sauce: quick, cheap and better than takeaway.

## Ingredients

- 280 g (1½ cups) jasmine rice
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) neutral oil
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 500 g frozen mixed vegetables
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) soy sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) sesame oil

## Method

1. Cook the rice, following the packet, then spread it on a tray to cool and dry while you get everything else ready.
2. Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a wok over high heat, scramble the eggs until just set, then tip them out.
3. Add the rest of the oil and stir-fry the frozen vegetables for 3–4 minutes, until hot.
4. Add the rice and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes, pressing it against the wok, until hot and starting to crisp.
5. Stir in the eggs, spring onions, soy sauce and sesame oil, and serve.

Friday fish and chips without the queue: crumbed fish and oven chips from the freezer, with peas and lemon.

## Ingredients

- 8 frozen crumbed fish fillets
- 1 kg frozen oven chips
- 280 g (2 cups) frozen peas
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Tartare sauce, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oven, following the packets, and bake the chips and fish on separate trays, adding the fish so they finish together.
2. A few minutes before they're ready, cook the peas in boiling water or the microwave for 3 minutes, then drain.
3. Serve with lemon wedges and tartare sauce.

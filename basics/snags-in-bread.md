The sausage sizzle at home: snags and soft onions folded into a slice of white bread with plenty of tomato sauce.

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) neutral oil
- 2 brown onions, thinly sliced
- 8 thin beef sausages
- 8 slices white bread
- Tomato sauce, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat and cook the onions for about 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and golden. Tip them into a bowl.
2. Cook the sausages in the same pan for 10–12 minutes, turning often, until browned and cooked through.
3. Lay each sausage on a slice of bread, top with onions and tomato sauce, and fold it up.

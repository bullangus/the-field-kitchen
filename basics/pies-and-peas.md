A meat pie from the freezer with peas and a good squirt of tomato sauce: the footy classic, at the kitchen table.

## Ingredients

- 4 frozen meat pies
- 280 g (2 cups) frozen peas
- Tomato sauce, to serve

## Method

1. Bake the pies from frozen, following the packet, until the pastry is golden and the filling is hot right through.
2. A few minutes before they're ready, cook the peas in boiling water or the microwave for 3 minutes, then drain.
3. Serve the pies with the peas and tomato sauce.

A tray of corn chips under refried beans, salsa and melted cheese, finished with sour cream and avocado: everyone digs in from the middle.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (435 g) refried beans
- 1 jar (300 g) salsa
- 1 large bag (230 g) corn chips
- 200 g (2 cups) grated cheese
- 1 avocado, diced
- Sour cream, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan / 400°F / gas 6).
2. Warm the refried beans and half the salsa together in a small pan.
3. Spread the corn chips on a large tray, spoon over the beans and scatter with the cheese. Bake for 8–10 minutes, until the cheese melts.
4. Top with the rest of the salsa, the avocado and sour cream.

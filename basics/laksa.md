A jar of laksa paste, coconut milk and chicken: a big, spicy bowl of soup from the international aisle.

## Ingredients

- 1 jar (185 g) laksa paste
- 800 ml (2 × 400 ml cans) coconut milk
- 500 ml (2 cups) chicken stock
- 500 g chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 200 g rice vermicelli
- 100 g (1 cup) bean sprouts
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Fresh coriander, to serve

## Method

1. Fry the laksa paste in a large saucepan over medium heat for 2 minutes, stirring, until fragrant.
2. Stir in the coconut milk and chicken stock and bring to a simmer.
3. Add the chicken and simmer for 8–10 minutes, until cooked through.
4. Meanwhile, soak or cook the rice vermicelli, following the packet, and drain.
5. Divide the noodles between bowls, ladle over the soup and top with bean sprouts, coriander and a lime wedge.

Warm falafel, hummus and crunchy salad rolled up in soft wraps: a fresh, filling dinner that needs almost no cooking.

## Ingredients

- 2 packets (about 20) falafel
- 8 wraps
- 1 tub (200 g) hummus
- ½ cos lettuce, shredded
- 2 medium tomatoes, diced
- 1 cucumber, sliced

## Method

1. Warm the falafel in the oven or air fryer, following the packet.
2. Warm the wraps for a few seconds in a dry pan or the microwave.
3. Spread each wrap with hummus, add salad and falafel, and roll up.

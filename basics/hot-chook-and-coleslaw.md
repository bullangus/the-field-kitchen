cuisine: Australian

serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min
leftovers: The chicken keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days, off the bone, for sandwiches and lunches. Dressed coleslaw goes soggy, so dress only what you'll eat.
---

A hot chicken from the deli counter, a bag of coleslaw and soft rolls: dinner on the table before the chicken's cooled.

## Ingredients

- 1 hot roast chicken
- 1 coleslaw kit, with its dressing
- 6 bread rolls
- Butter, for the rolls

## Method

1. Pull the chicken off the bone, or carve it.
2. Toss the coleslaw with its dressing.
3. Butter the rolls and let everyone fill their own.

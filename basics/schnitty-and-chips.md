Crumbed chicken schnitzels from the meat section, oven chips and a salad: the pub favourite without leaving home.

## Ingredients

- 4 crumbed chicken schnitzels
- 1 kg frozen oven chips
- 1 bag salad mix
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

## Method

1. Bake the chips, following the packet.
2. Cook the schnitzels, following the packet, in the oven or a frying pan with a little oil, until golden and cooked through.
3. Serve with the salad and lemon wedges.

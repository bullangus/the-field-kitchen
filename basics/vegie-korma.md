A jar of korma sauce simmered with chickpeas and vegies, with rice and naan: a mild, creamy curry the whole table will eat.

## Ingredients

- 280 g (1½ cups) basmati rice
- 1 jar (485 g) korma simmer sauce
- 400 g tin chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 500 g frozen mixed vegetables
- 4 plain naan breads

## Method

1. Cook the rice, following the packet.
2. Warm the korma sauce in a large pan, add the chickpeas and frozen vegetables, and simmer for 10–12 minutes, until the vegetables are hot and tender.
3. Warm the naan, following the packet, and serve with the rice.

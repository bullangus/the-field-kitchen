Fresh ravioli and a tub of sauce from the fridge section: dinner in the time it takes the water to boil.

## Ingredients

- 625 g fresh ravioli
- 1 tub (425 g) fresh pasta sauce
- Grated parmesan, to serve

## Method

1. Cook the ravioli in a large pot of salted boiling water, following the packet.
2. Meanwhile, warm the sauce in a small pan.
3. Drain the ravioli, toss with the sauce and finish with parmesan.

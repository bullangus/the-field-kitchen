A bag of frozen dumplings, pan-fried until the bottoms crackle, with bok choy and plenty of chilli crisp.

## Ingredients

- 2 bags (about 40) frozen dumplings
- 2 bunches bok choy, halved lengthways
- Soy sauce, to serve
- Chilli crisp, to serve

## Method

1. Cook the dumplings from frozen, following the packet: pan-fried, steamed or boiled.
2. Meanwhile, steam or boil the bok choy for 2–3 minutes, until just tender.
3. Serve with soy sauce and chilli crisp for dipping.

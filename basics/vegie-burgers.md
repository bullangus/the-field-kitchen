Frozen vegie patties in toasted buns with cheese, lettuce, tomato and sauce: burger night with nothing to chop but a tomato.

## Ingredients

- 4 frozen vegie burger patties
- 4 burger buns, split
- 4 cheese slices
- ¼ iceberg lettuce, shredded
- 2 medium tomatoes, sliced
- Tomato sauce, to serve

## Method

1. Cook the patties, following the packet, in a frying pan or the oven, and lay a cheese slice on each for the last minute.
2. Toast the buns, cut side down, in a dry pan.
3. Build the burgers with lettuce, tomato, a patty and tomato sauce.

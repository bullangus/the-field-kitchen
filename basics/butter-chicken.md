A jar of sauce, a tray of chicken thighs and a pack of naan: a takeaway night from the supermarket.

## Ingredients

- 280 g (1½ cups) basmati rice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) neutral oil
- 500 g chicken thighs, diced
- 1 jar (485 g) butter chicken simmer sauce
- 4 plain naan breads

## Method

1. Cook the rice, following the packet.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat and brown the chicken for 5–6 minutes.
3. Pour in the sauce and simmer for 10–15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
4. Warm the naan, following the packet, and serve with the rice.

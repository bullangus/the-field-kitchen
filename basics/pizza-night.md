Two frozen pizzas and a bag of salad: no one has to cook, and everyone gets their favourite slice.

## Ingredients

- 2 frozen pizzas
- 1 bag salad mix

## Method

1. Bake the pizzas, following the packet, until the cheese bubbles and the base is crisp.
2. Tip the salad into a bowl and serve with the pizza.

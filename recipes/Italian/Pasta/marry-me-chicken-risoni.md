One pan, one bowl each, and a lot of happy silence at the table. Seared chicken thigh in a creamy sun-dried tomato and Parmesan sauce, with the risoni cooked straight in the pan so it soaks up every bit of flavour.

Everything happens in one pan, so the risoni soaks up all the flavour of the chicken, the sun-dried tomatoes and the stock as it cooks. The spinach wilts in right at the end. It's creamy, comforting and quick enough for any night of the week.

## Ingredients

- 6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 red onion
- 4 cloves garlic
- 280 g jar sliced sun-dried tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Small bunch fresh thyme
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 900 ml chicken stock
- 300 g risoni
- 200 ml cream
- 60 g finely grated Parmesan
- 60 g (1 small bag) baby spinach
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Dice each chicken thigh into 6 pieces. Roughly dice the red onion and mince the garlic. Finely slice the thyme. Finely dice the sun-dried tomatoes so they melt into the sauce. Set everything aside.
2. Add the oil to a cast-iron or heavy-based pan over high heat. While the pan heats, season the chicken with salt on all sides. Put the chicken in the pan in an even layer, season the top with cracked black pepper and sear for 3–4 minutes.
3. Once the chicken is golden brown on one side, take it out of the pan. It doesn't need to be cooked through yet.
4. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the onion, garlic and thyme and sweat for 1–2 minutes, until the onion has softened. Add the sun-dried tomatoes, oregano, smoked paprika and stock and stir to combine.
5. Once the stock begins to simmer, add the risoni and cook for 7–8 minutes, until most of the liquid has been absorbed and the risoni is al dente.
6. Add the seared chicken, cream and Parmesan and stir through over low heat. Put the spinach on top and leave it to wilt for 2 minutes without stirring.
7. Stir the spinach through, then taste and adjust the seasoning. Serve.

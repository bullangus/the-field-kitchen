Onion and garlic softening in the pan, then the hiss of mince hitting the heat: the sound of a weeknight sorted. This is spag bol the easy way, with beef mince simmered in two jars of Mutti pasta sauce and tossed through spaghetti.

It's the dinner nobody argues about. Mutti's sauce is just good Italian tomatoes, so it tastes like it has bubbled away all afternoon when it has only had twenty minutes. Make a double batch and freeze half: a tub of spag bol in the freezer is the best kind of insurance.

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 brown onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 500 g beef mince
- 800 g (2 × 400 g jars) Mutti pasta sauce, such as Basilico
- ½ tsp MSG
- Table salt and pepper, to taste
- 400 g spaghetti
- Grated parmesan, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a large, deep frying pan over medium heat. Cook the onion for about 5 minutes, until soft, then add the garlic and cook for 1 minute more.
2. Turn the heat up to high, add the beef mince and cook for 5–6 minutes, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, until browned.
3. Pour in the pasta sauce, stir in the MSG and simmer gently for 15–20 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in a large pot of well-salted boiling water until al dente, following the packet. Drain.
5. Toss the spaghetti through the sauce, or spoon the sauce over the top, and finish with parmesan.

## Notes

- For a soffritto, finely dice 1 carrot and 1 celery stalk and cook them with the onion for 8–10 minutes, until soft and sweet, before adding the garlic.

The sharp, sweet smell of rice vinegar hitting hot rice, and the steam rising off the bowl as you fold. Short-grain rice seasoned with rice vinegar, mirin, sugar and salt: the base for all our sushi. Make it first and let it cool to room temperature before shaping.

Good sushi rice is all in the folding. A cutting motion with the paddle coats every grain without mashing it, so the rice stays glossy and holds together when pressed. One batch makes about 12 pieces of Salmon Nigiri, Salmon Aburi or Chicken Avocado Nigiri.

## Ingredients

- 300 g (2 rice-cooker cups / 360 ml) sushi rice
- 60 ml (¼ cup) rice vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) mirin
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. Rinse the rice until the water runs almost clear, then cook it in a rice cooker or pot.
2. While the rice cooks, stir the rice vinegar, mirin, sugar and salt together until the sugar and salt dissolve.
3. While the rice is hot, tip it into a wide bowl and fold the seasoning through with a rice paddle, using a cutting motion so the grains don't get mashed.
4. Let it cool to room temperature before shaping.

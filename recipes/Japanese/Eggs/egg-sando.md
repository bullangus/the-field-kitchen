The humble Japanese convenience-store sandwich that's somehow better than it has any right to be. Creamy Kewpie egg salad is mounded into soft, crustless Shokupan and cut on the diagonal.

## Ingredients

- 6 slices shokupan (Japanese milk bread)
- Spreadable butter
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vinegar
- 5 large eggs
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) Kewpie mayonnaise
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) milk
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp sugar

## Method

### Egg salad

1. Bring a pot of water to a gentle boil, with enough water to cover the eggs. Add the vinegar. Lower the eggs in carefully with a spoon and boil for 10 minutes. Transfer them straight to an ice bath and cool for 5 minutes.
2. Peel the eggs and cut them in half. Separate the whites from the yolks. Finely mince the whites with a knife until small and even.
3. Put the yolks in a mixing bowl with the mayonnaise, milk, salt, pepper and sugar and mix until smooth. Gently fold in the minced whites. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

### Assemble

1. Spread a thin, even layer of butter on one side of each slice of shokupan. Spoon the egg salad onto one slice, mounding a little more towards the centre. Top with a second slice, buttered side down.
2. Trim off the crusts with a sharp knife. If you like, wrap the sandwich in baking paper and refrigerate for 5–10 minutes so the filling sets and doesn't spill when cut. Cut diagonally and serve.

Floured, chilled and fried until crisp, then tossed in the classic butter and hot sauce mix.

## Ingredients

- 65 g (½ cup) plain (all-purpose) flour
- ¼ tsp ground paprika
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
- ¼ tsp salt
- 10 chicken wings
- About 480 ml (2 cups) vegetable oil, for frying

### For the sauce

- 57 g (¼ cup) butter
- 60 ml (¼ cup) hot sauce
- 1 pinch ground black pepper
- 1 pinch garlic powder
- ½ tsp MSG

## Method

1. Whisk the flour, paprika, cayenne and salt together in a large bowl.
2. Add the wings and toss until evenly coated. Arrange them in a single layer in a 23 × 33 cm (9 × 13 inch) glass baking dish, cover and refrigerate for 1–1½ hours.
3. Pour about 2.5 cm (1 inch) of oil into a deep, heavy frying pan, just enough to cover the wings, and heat to 190°C (375°F).
4. Meanwhile, for the sauce, put the butter, hot sauce, pepper, garlic powder and MSG in a small saucepan over low heat. Stir until the butter melts and everything is well combined. Take off the heat and set aside.
5. Fry the wings for 10–15 minutes, until they start to crisp and brown, keeping the oil at temperature. Drain on paper towel or a wire rack.
6. Drizzle the sauce over the wings on a platter, or toss them together in a bowl until coated.

## Notes

- 2026-10-28: Added ½ tsp MSG to the hot sauce butter. We add it to most savoury dishes.

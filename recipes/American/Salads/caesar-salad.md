Our Caesar: crisp cos, bacon, shredded chicken thigh, jammy eggs and garlic-butter croutons, tossed in Beerenberg Caesar dressing and finished with plenty of parmesan.

## Ingredients

- 200 g streaky bacon
- 2 heads cos lettuce
- 4 chicken thighs (about 500 g)
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil
- Salt and pepper
- ½ tsp MSG
- 4 eggs
- 125 ml (½ cup) Caesar dressing (Beerenberg is our favourite)
- 40 g parmesan, for grating over

### For the croutons

- 3 slices bread (any kind)
- 30 g (2 tbsp) butter, melted
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning

## Method

### Prepare everything

1. Fry the bacon until crisp, then chop it.
2. Wash the cos lettuce, dry it well and roughly chop.
3. Season the chicken with the oil, salt, pepper and MSG. Cook in a frying pan over medium-high heat until golden and cooked through, about 5–6 minutes each side, then shred.
4. Bring a pot of water to the boil, gently lower in the eggs and boil for 7 minutes. Cool them in iced water, then peel and quarter.
5. Cut the bread into cubes. Toss with the melted butter, garlic and Italian seasoning, then air-fry at 180°C for 5 minutes, until golden and crisp.

### Assemble

1. Toss the lettuce, bacon and chicken with the dressing.
2. Pile onto plates, add the eggs and croutons, then grate plenty of parmesan over the top.

## Notes

- The eggs are cooked the same way as the Ramen Eggs, just for 7 minutes.

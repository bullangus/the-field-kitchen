Pan-fried salmon in a sticky honey, soy, garlic and ginger glaze. Serve with rice and greens.

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets
- 16 g (2 tbsp) cornflour (cornstarch)
- Steamed rice and vegetables such as broccolini, to serve

### For the sauce

- 75 ml (5 tbsp) honey
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oyster sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 15 g fresh ginger, minced
- 1 red chilli (use more or less depending on how hot you like it)

## Method

1. Pour hot water over the salmon skin until it shrinks slightly, then peel the skin off. Coat the skinless fillets evenly in the cornflour.
2. Put the salmon in a hot pan and fry over medium heat until golden brown on both sides, about 6–10 minutes.
3. Combine all the sauce ingredients, add them to the pan with the salmon and cook until the sauce has thickened and the salmon is glazed, about 5 minutes.
4. Serve with rice and vegetables such as broccolini.

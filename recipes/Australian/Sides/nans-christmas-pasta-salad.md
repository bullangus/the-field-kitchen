Nan's pasta salad with kabana and thousand island dressing, a side we have every Christmas. Serve it warm or cool.

## Ingredients

- 500 g spiral pasta (fusilli), or any shape you like
- 2 kabana sticks
- 220 ml (⅔ of a 330 ml bottle) thousand island dressing

## Method

1. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions.
2. While the pasta cooks, slice the kabana into half moons.
3. Drain the pasta and put it in a large bowl. Add the kabana and enough thousand island dressing to coat everything, about ⅔ of the bottle, and toss to combine.
4. Serve warm, or leave to cool and serve at room temperature or cold.

Creamy, nutty and warming, the satay you'll want to mop up every last drop of. Strips of chicken thigh are simmered in a quick peanut and red curry sauce. Serve with steamed rice.

The sauce is rich and creamy, with a gentle warmth from the curry paste and a squeeze of lime to brighten it. It comes together quickly, so it's an easy weeknight favourite. Spoon plenty of sauce over the rice.

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken thigh fillets, cut into strips
- 400 ml can coconut milk
- 95 g (6 tbsp) peanut butter
- 3–4 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lime juice
- Salt, to taste
- Steamed rice, to serve

## Method

1. Cook the chicken strips briefly in a frying pan, then set aside.
2. Put the coconut milk, peanut butter and curry paste in a small saucepan and bring to the boil. Use more or less curry paste depending on how spicy you like it.
3. Add the chicken and simmer until the sauce comes together and thickens and the chicken is cooked through.
4. Season with the lime juice and salt to taste. Serve with steamed rice.

## Notes

- The sauce freezes well, so leftovers can go in a small jar in the freezer.

A mild, fragrant coconut curry with chicken, potato and carrot. Serve over steamed jasmine rice.

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil, or thick coconut cream from the top of the can
- 4–5 tbsp yellow curry paste (a good authentic brand, or homemade)
- 500 g (1 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 400 ml (14 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- A splash of chicken stock or water
- 3 Dutch cream, Désirée or Warba potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1 brown (yellow) onion, cut into wedges
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fish sauce
- ½ tsp MSG
- 1–2½ tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- Fresh coriander and sliced red chillies, to garnish
- Steamed jasmine rice, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oil or coconut cream in a large pot or wok over medium heat. Add the curry paste and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes, until fragrant and the oils start to separate.
2. Add the chicken and stir to coat every piece in the paste. Cook for about 2 minutes, then sprinkle in the curry powder.
3. Pour in the coconut milk and a splash of stock or water and bring to a gentle boil.
4. Add the potatoes, onion and carrots, and season with the fish sauce, palm sugar and MSG.
5. Cover, turn down to a simmer and cook for 10–15 minutes, until the potatoes are fork-tender and the chicken is cooked through.
6. Taste and balance with more fish sauce or sugar if needed. Garnish with coriander and chilli and serve hot over jasmine rice.

## Notes

- Homemade yellow curry paste is made from dried chillies, lemongrass, galangal, garlic, shallots, turmeric, cumin, coriander and yellow curry powder.
- Yukon gold potatoes aren't sold in Australia; Dutch cream or Désirée are the closest, waxy all-rounders.

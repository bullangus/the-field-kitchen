A cool, bright spoonful that lifts whatever it sits beside. Our simple, tangy yoghurt sauce is just yoghurt, lemon, salt and pepper, mixed to taste.

We make it by taste and feel rather than measurements, adding more lemon or salt until it's just right. It's the sauce we reach for alongside grilled meat, roast vegetables or anything that needs a bit of brightness. Keep a bowl of it on the table.

## Ingredients

- 250 g (1 cup) Greek yoghurt
- 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) lemon juice (about ½ lemon)
- ¼ tsp salt
- A few grinds of black pepper

## Method

1. Mix the yoghurt, lemon juice, salt and pepper together.
2. Taste and adjust: more lemon for tang, more salt to make it pop.

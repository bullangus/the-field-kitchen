Packet ramen dressed up: smoking-hot oil poured over the seasoning and garlic, then soy, black vinegar and a crispy fried egg.

## Ingredients

- 1 packet spicy instant ramen noodles
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 spring onion, green part only, finely sliced
- 1 egg
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) peanut oil
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Chinese black vinegar

## Method

1. Bring a pot of water to the boil. Cook the noodles for 1 minute less than the packet says.
2. In the bowl you'll serve the noodles in, put as much of the noodles' seasoning packet as you like, and finely grate in the garlic. Leave out the dried vegetable packet: it clumps in the oil.
3. While the noodles cook, fry the egg in 15 ml (1 tbsp) of the peanut oil. Heat the remaining oil in a small saucepan until it just starts to smoke, then pour it over the seasoning and garlic in the bowl. Stir well, then add the soy sauce and vinegar.
4. Drain the noodles and add them to the bowl. Toss well to coat them in the spicy oil, then top with the spring onion and the fried egg.

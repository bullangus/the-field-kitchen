A mountain of red chillies that looks fierce and tastes addictive. Crisp, double-fried chicken is tossed through toasted dried chillies and tingling Sichuan pepper. It's not as spicy as it looks, and it disappears fast.

The chillies are there for their smoky warmth and fragrance as much as their heat, and you pick around them as you eat. The Sichuan pepper brings that tingle that keeps you going back for more. Serve it in the middle of the table and watch it disappear.

## Ingredients

### For the chicken

- 2–4 chicken thighs
- 2.5 cm (1 inch) piece fresh ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Shaoxing wine
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- Cornflour (cornstarch), enough to coat the chicken
- Neutral oil, for deep-frying

### For the wok

- 3 cups dried Sichuan red chillies
- 2 tbsp Sichuan red peppercorns
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2.5 cm (1 inch) piece fresh ginger, minced
- 3 spring onions (scallions), chopped
- A splash of neutral oil

### For the seasoning

- 1 tsp salt
- 4 tbsp (¼ cup) Sichuan chilli flakes
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp MSG

### To serve

- Sesame seeds and sliced spring onions, to garnish

## Method

1. Cut the chicken thighs into 2.5 cm (1 inch) cubes. Mix them with the Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, ginger and garlic until combined, then marinate in the fridge for 1 hour.
2. Cut the dried chillies into thirds and remove as many of the seeds as you can.
3. Mince the ginger and garlic for the wok, and chop the spring onions.
4. Mix the salt, chilli flakes, sugar and MSG together for the seasoning.
5. When you're ready to fry, toss the marinated chicken in enough cornflour to coat it.
6. Heat the oil for deep-frying to 160°C (325°F). Fry the chicken for about 3 minutes, then lift it out. Raise the oil to 190°C (375°F) and fry the chicken again until golden brown.
7. Toast the Sichuan peppercorns in a dry wok until they start to smell toasty. Add the splash of oil and the garlic, ginger and spring onions and stir-fry until very fragrant, then add the dried chillies and stir-fry for a couple more minutes.
8. Add the chicken, then plenty of the seasoning mix, and toss to coat.
9. Garnish with sesame seeds and more spring onion, and serve.

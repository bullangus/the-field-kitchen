A cooling yoghurt and mint side with ginger and garam masala for warmth. We serve it with Moh's Duck and Tamarind Curry.

## Ingredients

- 250 g (1 cup) Greek yoghurt
- 1 garlic clove
- Thumb-sized piece ginger, finely grated
- ¼ small pack (UK size) mint, leaves chopped
- Juice of ½ lime
- ½ tsp garam masala, plus a little extra to serve
- ¼ cucumber, grated and lightly squeezed

## Method

1. Mix all the ingredients together and season.
2. Serve sprinkled with extra garam masala.

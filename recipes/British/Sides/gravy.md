No roast is finished until the gravy jug goes round. This one is made in minutes from the Roast Chicken's pan juices, thickened with Gravox. Makes about 1 cup.

It's simple and it's quick, but it's what ties a roast together. Making it from the chicken's own juices means it tastes of the meal it's served with. Pour it generously over the chicken and potatoes, and keep the jug close by.

## Ingredients

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) pan juices from the Roast Chicken
- 1½ tbsp Gravox Traditional Gravy Mix
- 185 ml (¾ cup) water

## Method

1. While the chicken rests, stand it up over the roasting tray so the juices in the cavity drain into the pan juices.
2. In a small saucepan, mix the pan juices and Gravox into a smooth paste.
3. Cook over low heat for 30 seconds, stirring continuously.
4. Gradually add the water, stirring as you go.
5. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 1 minute, stirring occasionally.

## Notes

- For thicker or thinner gravy, use more or less Gravox.

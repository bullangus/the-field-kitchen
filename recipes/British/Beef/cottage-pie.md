Beef, carrot and pea filling under buttery mash, finished with a mozzarella and Parmesan top. Made and baked in one ovenproof pan.

## Ingredients

### For the mash

- 4 washed potatoes, peeled and chopped
- Salt and pepper
- 56 g (¼ cup) butter, cut into chunks
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) milk or cream

### For the filling

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 450 g (1 lb) beef mince
- 1 medium brown onion, diced
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 16 g (2 tbsp) plain (all-purpose) flour
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) tomato sauce (ketchup) or tomato paste
- 360 ml (1½ cups) chicken stock
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire sauce
- 70 g (½ cup) frozen peas
- MSG, to taste

### For the top

- A handful of grated mozzarella
- A handful of finely grated Parmesan

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan / 400°F / gas 6).
2. Put the potatoes in a medium pot and cover with cold water. Add ½ tsp salt and bring to a simmer. Cook until fork-tender, about 10–15 minutes. Drain and set aside.
3. While the potatoes cook, heat the olive oil in a 25 cm (10 inch) ovenproof frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the beef and ¼ tsp salt. Cook, breaking the meat into crumbles as you go, until well browned. Transfer the beef to a bowl with a slotted spoon and set aside.
4. Spoon off and discard all but a couple of teaspoons of fat from the pan. Turn the heat to medium, add the onion and carrots and cook until the onion begins to soften, about 5 minutes.
5. Add the garlic and Italian seasoning and cook for 30 seconds.
6. Stir in the flour and tomato sauce or paste and cook for 1 minute. Stir in the stock and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, for 3–6 minutes, until thickened.
7. Take the pan off the heat and season with salt, pepper and MSG to taste. Stir in the beef and the frozen peas.
8. Add the butter to the potatoes and mash until smooth. Stir in the milk or cream and season with salt and pepper to taste.
9. Spoon the mash over the filling in the pan and spread it out evenly.
10. Bake for 30 minutes. Take it out of the oven, scatter over the mozzarella and Parmesan, and return it to the oven for 15 minutes.
11. Cool for 10–15 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- No ovenproof frying pan? Do the stovetop steps in any large frying pan, then spread the filling in a 20 or 23 cm (8 or 9 inch) square baking dish and top with the mash.
- 2026-11-10: Our changes to the original: 4 washed potatoes instead of 2 large russets (about 340 g / 12 oz each), no egg yolk in the mash, 1 tsp Italian seasoning instead of ⅛ tsp ground thyme, MSG with the seasoning, double the tomato sauce or paste, and a mozzarella and Parmesan top for the last 15 minutes of a 45-minute bake (the original bakes for 35–45 minutes).

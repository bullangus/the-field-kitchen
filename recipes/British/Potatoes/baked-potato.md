A baked potato with a crisp, salty skin and a fluffy middle is one of life's simple pleasures. These are baked in foil until soft, crisped with olive oil and salt, then quartered and piled high with whatever toppings you love.

It's a meal that's as easy or as indulgent as you want it to be. Melting garlic butter and cheese, a spoonful of garlic mushrooms, crispy bacon, coleslaw, chives, spring onion or corn: set the toppings out and let everyone build their own. The crispy, salty skin is the best bit.

## Ingredients

- 4 large potatoes
- Olive oil spray
- Salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan / 425°F / gas 7).
2. Wash the potatoes and dry them completely, then wrap each one in foil.
3. Bake the potatoes in their foil for 1½ hours.
4. Take them out of the oven and remove the foil. Spray them with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
5. Return them to the oven for 15–20 minutes.
6. Take them out of the oven, quarter each potato and pile on whatever toppings you like. Pick from the Topping Options and set them out so everyone can build their own.

## Topping Options

### Garlic butter

- 30 g (2 tbsp) butter
- 1 clove garlic

1. Crush the garlic into the butter and melt it in the microwave. Spoon it over the hot potatoes.

### Cheese

- Grated cheese

1. Scatter it over the hot potatoes so it melts.

### Garlic mushrooms

- Mushrooms, sliced
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- A knob of butter

1. Put the mushrooms in a dry frying pan, with no butter or oil, over medium-high heat. Let them sweat and give up their liquid until it cooks off and they begin to caramelise.
2. Add the butter and garlic and toss until the butter has melted and the garlic is fragrant. Spoon over the potatoes.

Shortcut: use a knob of premade garlic butter (shop-bought, or the Garlic butter topping) in place of the butter and garlic.

### Bacon

- Streaky bacon, chopped

1. Put the bacon in a cold frying pan, then turn the heat to medium. Fry, stirring now and then, until crisp, 6–8 minutes. Drain on paper towel.

### Coleslaw

- Coleslaw

1. Spoon it onto the potatoes just before eating.

### Chives

- Fresh chives

1. Finely snip the chives with scissors and scatter over the potatoes.

### Spring onion

- Spring onions

1. Thinly slice the spring onions and scatter over the potatoes.

### Corn

- Corn kernels

1. Warm the corn and spoon it over the potatoes.

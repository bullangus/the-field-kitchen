A baked potato with a crisp, salty skin and a fluffy middle is one of life's simple pleasures. These are baked in foil until soft, crisped with olive oil and salt, then quartered and piled high with whatever toppings you love.

It's a meal that's as easy or as indulgent as you want it to be. Melting garlic butter and cheese, a spoonful of garlic mushrooms, crispy bacon, coleslaw or corn: set the toppings out and let everyone build their own. The crispy, salty skin is the best bit.

## Ingredients

- 4 large potatoes
- Olive oil spray
- Salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan / 425°F / gas 7).
2. Wash the potatoes and dry them completely, then wrap each one in foil.
3. Bake the potatoes in their foil for 1½ hours.
4. Take them out of the oven and remove the foil. Spray them with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
5. Return them to the oven for 15–20 minutes.
6. Take them out of the oven, quarter each potato and top with whatever you like: garlic butter, cheese, garlic mushrooms, bacon, coleslaw, corn and so on.

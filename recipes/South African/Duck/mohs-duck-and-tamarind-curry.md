title: Moh's Duck and Tamarind Curry
cuisine: South African

serves: 4
source: Mohammed Khalid Moola, from a cookbook (p. 43)

A modern Durban duck curry from Mohammed Khalid Moola of Ladysmith, with a nod to France in the orange sauce and his mother's touch of tamarind.

## Ingredients

- 4 duck breasts
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) sunflower oil
- 30 g (80 ml / ⅓ cup) gram (chickpea) flour
- Salt
- White pepper

### For the curry sauce

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) oil
- 2 tbsp coriander seeds
- 2 tbsp cumin seeds
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 2 large onions, chopped
- Handful of curry leaves
- 3 tbsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 85 g (1 cup) desiccated coconut
- 150 g (1 cup) cashew nuts
- 1 tsp turmeric

### To finish

- 750 ml (3 cups) water
- 250 ml (1 cup) orange juice
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) tamarind paste
- Fresh coriander, to garnish

## Method

1. Bring the duck breasts to room temperature and pat them dry. Score the skin and fat in a diamond pattern.
2. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan / 400°F / gas 6).
3. Dust the duck with the gram flour, salt and white pepper.
4. Heat the sunflower oil in a large frying pan until hot and sear the duck on all sides.
5. Transfer to an oven tray and roast for about 10 minutes.
6. For the curry sauce, heat the oil in a heavy-based pot. Add the coriander, cumin, sesame and mustard seeds and cook until the seeds pop.
7. Add the onions and keep cooking until softened. Add the curry leaves, chilli flakes and garam masala.
8. After 2–3 minutes, add the coconut, then the cashews and turmeric. Cook for 5–7 minutes.
9. Set aside to cool, then blend into a smooth paste.
10. Return the paste to the pot and add the roasted duck, skin side up. Pour in the water and orange juice, and add the sugar and tamarind. Cover and cook for about 20–30 minutes, until the duck is very tender.
11. Scatter with fresh coriander. Serve with rice, Simple Naan and Raita (both in the Indian section).

## Notes

- The cups are South African (250 ml).
- 2026-10-28: We use 4 duck breasts in place of the book's 2 ducks, jointed, and keep every other quantity the same.
- 2026-10-28: The book just says to brown the duck for a few minutes and roast it for 20 minutes, with no oven temperature. We bring the breasts to room temperature, dry and score them, sear them on all sides, roast them at 180°C fan for about 10 minutes, then cook them covered and skin side up in the sauce.

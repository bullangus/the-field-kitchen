A taste of the braai: smoky fire, good company and this relish spooned over everything. A simple, full-flavoured South African tomato and onion relish for pap, boerewors rolls, meatballs or even pasta.

It's the relish that goes on everything at a braai: piled onto boerewors rolls, spooned over pap or served alongside grilled meat. Sweet, tangy and full of flavour, it's simple to make. Mrs Ball's chutney gives it its unmistakable taste.

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 2 onions (or 1 large onion), roughly chopped
- 2 tins diced tomatoes (or 8 fresh tomatoes, diced)
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Mrs Ball's chutney
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce
- ½ tsp MSG
- Salt and pepper
- 12 g (1 tbsp) sugar (optional), to help the caramelising

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan and fry the chunky onion pieces over medium heat until soft and caramelised.
2. Add the tomatoes, Mrs Ball's chutney, sugar, balsamic vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, MSG, salt and pepper.
3. Simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
